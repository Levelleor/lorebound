# Optional Mature Reproductive Anatomy Fields

## Idea
Allow per-story mature (18+) settings to enable optional reproductive and sex-related anatomy fields.

## Why consider it?
Some mature RP scenarios require additional state.

## Rough behaviour
Keep gender identity distinct from anatomy and exclude mature-only fields when disabled; general injuries remain available.

## Variations and alternatives
Mature/NSFW/R labels; built-in or custom fields.

## Open questions and risks
- Should disabling the option hide or erase existing stored data?

## Evaluation notes
- Origin: **user**. User-requested direction; exact mechanics remain to be reviewed.
- These are rough ideas, not approved requirements or implemented features.

## Decision history

Additional mature-specific toggles, separate fields or visibility settings remain undecided. Keep this candidate in Inbox for reconsideration if needed.
