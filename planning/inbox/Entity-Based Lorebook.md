# Entity-Based Lorebook

## Idea
Store lore about PCs, NPCs, settlements, world regions, factions, species, objects and abilities.

## Why consider it?
World knowledge must outlive any individual prose passage.

## Rough behaviour
Link stable lore entries to mutable character/location/item entities rather than copying live states.

## Variations and alternatives
Typed entries versus freeform text.

## Open questions and risks
- How are rumours distinguished from established facts?
