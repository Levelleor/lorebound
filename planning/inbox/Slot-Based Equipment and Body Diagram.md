# Slot-Based Equipment and Body Diagram

## Idea
Show each character's worn/held equipment by body slot with text labels and a small anatomy diagram.

## Why consider it?
Makes equipped gear legible and available to AI context.

## Rough behaviour
Support left/right hand, head, torso, feet, accessories, layering and custom slots; separate equipment from inventory.

## Variations and alternatives
Static schematic now, images later.

## Open questions and risks
- What should happen when equipment becomes anatomically incompatible?
