# Manual Generation Branches

## Idea
Allow manually selected alternative story heads and their associated world-state histories.

## Why consider it?
Players can explore different outcomes without losing the original version.

## Rough behaviour
Branches share earlier history and track divergent prose/events.

## Variations and alternatives
Defer branching in favour of simple undo and retry.

## Open questions and risks
- Do not introduce automatic downstream rewrites after old edits.
