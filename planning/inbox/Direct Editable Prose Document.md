# Direct Editable Prose Document

## Idea
Allow editing, inserting and deleting text directly inside earlier story messages.

## Why consider it?
The storytelling surface should work like a prose editor, not an immutable chat transcript.

## Rough behaviour
Use stable text-block identities and editable passages retaining original turn provenance.

## Variations and alternatives
ProseMirror-style document versus individually editable turn bodies.

## Open questions and risks
- How should block IDs survive split, merge and reorder operations?
