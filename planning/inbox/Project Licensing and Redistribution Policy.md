# Project Licensing and Redistribution Policy

## Idea
Choose a licence matching local downloads, personal modifications and the desire to restrict unauthorised reuse.

## Why consider it?
Permissive licences allow competitors; proprietary terms may block normal user mods.

## Rough behaviour
Compare GPL/AGPL, all-rights-reserved and source-available terms without assuming any was selected.

## Variations and alternatives
A custom permission grant may suit local use while reserving commercial rights.

## Open questions and risks
- Exactly what copying, redistribution, plugin development and commercial use should be permitted?
