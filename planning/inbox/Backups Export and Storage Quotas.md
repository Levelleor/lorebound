# Backups Export and Storage Quotas

## Idea
Provide robust backup, restore, usage estimation and quota handling for browser-owned data.

## Why consider it?
Clearing site data or exceeding quota should not silently destroy adventures.

## Rough behaviour
Offer full-library and single-story exports, import validation, optional persistent-storage request and quota dashboard.

## Variations and alternatives
Automatic filesystem backup where browser permissions allow.

## Open questions and risks
- What backup location and cadence can work without a server?
