# Freeform Player Action Modes

## Idea
Provide Do, Say, Story and Continue as distinct ways of interacting with the AI.

## Why consider it?
Player actions, dialogue and direct prose authorship have different narrative semantics.

## Rough behaviour
Offer mode-specific prompts, free-text input and a hands-off Continue action.

## Variations and alternatives
A single input field could initially cover several modes.

## Open questions and risks
- Which modes should be capable of authoritatively changing world state?
