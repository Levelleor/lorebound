# Automatic Long-Term Story Summaries

## Idea
Independently summarise older history into persistent, rebuildable memory.

## Why consider it?
Long stories exceed model context windows.

## Rough behaviour
Summaries record source ranges and are invalidated after relevant historical edits.

## Variations and alternatives
Rolling summary versus hierarchical scene/chapter summaries.

## Open questions and risks
- How will the summariser avoid promoting failed attempts into facts?
