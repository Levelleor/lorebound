# Retry Undo and Alternative Continuations

## Idea
Support response retry, undo/redo and a choice of alternative generated continuations.

## Why consider it?
Players can refine undesired AI turns while retaining their story.

## Rough behaviour
Link generated variants to their provisional state events and restore state on undo.

## Variations and alternatives
Simple retry versus retained generation history.

## Open questions and risks
- Should alternate output choices be available for old turns?

- Origin: **agent**. Assistant-proposed option, not a confirmed user preference.
