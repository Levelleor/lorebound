# Single-Player Account-Free Experience

## Idea
Make Lorebound a single-user local-first story application without account registration or multiplayer.

## Why consider it?
Low-friction personal use without central identity infrastructure.

## Rough behaviour
Store each adventure independently in the user's local browser.

## Variations and alternatives
Optional multiple local profiles is a separate possibility.

## Open questions and risks
- Should locally shared devices support library isolation?
